One product only:

- **€149/month**;
- **21-day trial**;
- **€0 during trial**;

- per location;

## Current architecture

```text
acquisition → D1/audit → report → activation/onboarding → Climbo
```

- **D1** owns lead, consent, attribution, commercial lifecycle, provenance and binding.
- **Climbo** is downstream delivery after activation/binding.
- Pre-sale prospect != Climbo contact.
- No GHL, parallel CRM or custom customer portal in the Lean MVP.
- API where possible; browser automation only as last mile after a real dry-run proves the need.

---

## Vertical model

Beauty & Wellness is the first active campaign vertical, not a separate product.

A vertical is a **configuration / campaign layer** of the same Audit Engine, lifecycle and product.

Real non-Beauty prospects may be served as founder-assisted pilots using the master Trovatemi product without opening a new vertical campaign.

## Review policy

Trovatemi uses **neutral, universal review solicitation**.

Do not adopt vendor/community patterns that intentionally route:

- satisfied customers → public review;
- dissatisfied customers → private feedback;

for the purpose of suppressing negative public reviews.

No review incentives.

Vendor SEO claims remain evidence until independently verified; do not turn them into guaranteed ranking claims.

---

## Report / audit

**Reportly is out.**

Trovatemi report is generated by the Trovatemi Audit Engine using:

- verifiable public evidence;
- controlled cohort / benchmark;
- five private answers;
- deterministic, versioned scoring;
- Trovatemi explanation;
- trial / activation CTA.

LLMs may explain; they do not invent the score or Maps ranking.

## Delivery / onboarding

Current direction is assisted, standardized activation rather than premature full self-service.

- preconfigure what can be preconfigured;
- client handles OAuth / consent where required;
- recurring delivery should be automated where possible;
- recurring manual service is not the core product;
- no new portal until a real Climbo dry-run proves a specific need.

C0.1B classifies each setup step as:

`PRESET / API / UI-AUTO / CLIENTE / UMANO / ELIMINARE`

Before any tool, integration, purchase or side quest, disclose:

1. upfront cost;
2. recurring cost;
3. mandatory downstream cost;
4. free/incomplete-tier limitations;
